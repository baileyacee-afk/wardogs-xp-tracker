# Privacy policy

For Wardogs XP Tracker and its Microsoft Store version, Match Bonus Tracker. Neither one collects, sends or
shares any data.

- **What it reads:** while the game is running, it takes screenshots of parts of the game window and reads
  the XP and cash numbers with Windows' built-in text recognition, which runs on your PC. It never reads
  the game's memory or files.
- **What it keeps:** your match results, settings, a log, and a few pictures of the game screen used to
  check its readings. All of it stays in the app's data folder on your PC (tray menu: Open data folder),
  and matches you save go to your Documents folder. Uninstalling the Store version removes its data folder.
- **What it sends:** nothing. The app makes no network connections. The only links it opens are this
  project's GitHub pages, in your browser, when you click them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/baileyacee-afk/wardogs-xp-tracker/issues.
